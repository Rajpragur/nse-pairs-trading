# Data Source, Rights and Coverage Checklist (local)

No separate vendor permission letter is a repository-level prerequisite: the intended ingestion route is that the user pulls data through their own broker account. This checklist is not legal advice and does not assume API access permits every use. Record the actual source/product and check applicable account/data terms for access, local retention, private analysis, and planned derived-result sharing/publication. Raw market data remain local and gitignored; never commit or redistribute them without explicit permission.

Before use, record in the local manifest: provider/product, account/API product name (never credentials), retrieval time, coverage dates, checksum, schema/parser version, raw/adjusted status, corporate-action source/factors, and universe provenance. Verify instrument master mapping and historical coverage. Do not claim complete historical coverage unless delisted/renamed securities and gaps are addressed.
